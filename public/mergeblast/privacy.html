Privacy Policy — Merge Blast

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Merge Blast ("the Game") is published by Afterwish, Inc. ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the Game collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

- Game progress (levels, scores, coins, outfits, settings) is stored only on your device. The Game has no account system and does not send your progress to our servers.

- Friend codes: your friend code contains your player name, level and best score. It is shared only when you copy it and send it to someone yourself.

- Purchases: payments are handled by Apple, Google or Huawei. The Game receives confirmation of the purchase only, never your payment details.

- Advertising: the Game shows optional rewarded ads and occasional full-screen ads between games through Google AdMob (App Store and Google Play versions) and Petal Ads (HUAWEI Ads, AppGallery version). These providers may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address and ad interactions to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU/UK), you are asked for consent first.

- Petal Ads (AppGallery version): Petal Ads runs only after you agree in the Game; if you decline, no ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings. The Game asks Petal Ads for non-personalised ads only, without location. To serve, measure and protect these ads, Huawei processes device information (such as the Open Advertising ID (OAID), device model and operating system), network information (such as IP address, network type and carrier), app information (app name, version and language) and ad events (impressions, clicks, video plays). Huawei is an independent controller of this data; see the Statement About HUAWEI Ads and Privacy linked below. On Huawei devices you can reset your OAID or turn off personalised ads in your device's privacy or ads settings.

How we use information

To keep your progress, deliver purchased items, show ads and keep the Game working. We do not sell your personal information.
