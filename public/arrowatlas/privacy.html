Privacy Policy — Arrow Atlas

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Arrow Atlas - Treasure Puzzle ("the Game") is published by Afterwish, Inc. ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the Game collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

- Game progress (levels, stars, coins, gems, boosters, settings) is stored on your device.

- Sign-in and cloud save (optional): if you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive a user identifier from Apple or Google and, if you choose to share it, your name. We then keep a copy of your game progress on our server (hosted by Cloudflare) so you can continue on another device. We do not receive your email address if you hide it, your password, contacts or anything else from your Apple or Google account.

- Purchases: payments are handled by Apple, Google or Huawei. We receive confirmation of the purchase only, never your card details.

- Advertising: the Game shows ads through Google AdMob (App Store and Google Play versions) and Petal Ads (HUAWEI Ads, AppGallery version). These providers may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device and network information and ad interactions to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU/UK), you are asked for consent first. The iOS version does not track you across other companies' apps and websites.

- Petal Ads (AppGallery version): Petal Ads only runs after you agree in the Game, and you can change your choice in Settings. The Game asks for non-personalised ads only, without location. To serve, measure and protect these ads, Huawei processes device information (such as the Open Advertising ID (OAID), device model and operating system), network information (such as IP address, network type and carrier), app information and ad events. Huawei is an independent controller of this data; see the Statement About HUAWEI Ads and Privacy linked below.

How we use information

To save and restore your progress, deliver purchased items, show ads and keep the Game working and secure. We do not sell your personal information and do not use it for any other purpose.

Retention and deletion

Your account and cloud save are kept until you delete them. Delete them at any time in the Game: Settings → Delete account → Delete. They are removed from our server immediately. See Delete account for other ways.
