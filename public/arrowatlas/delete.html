Delete your Arrow Atlas account

In the game: Settings → Delete account → Delete. This removes your account (your Apple or Google user identifier and name) and your cloud save from our server immediately.

You can also email support@afterwishinc.com from any device with the subject "Delete account" and the sign-in method you used (Apple or Google); we delete the data within 7 days.

Progress stored on your phone stays there until you uninstall the Game.

Purchase records are kept by Apple, Google or Huawei under their own policies.
