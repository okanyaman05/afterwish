Privacy Policy — Jelly Sugar Match

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Jelly Sugar Match ("the Game") is published by Afterwish, Inc. ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the Game collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

- Game progress (levels, stars, coins, settings) is stored on your device. If you sign in, your player name, level and star totals are sent to our leaderboard server.

- Sign-in (optional): if you sign in with Facebook or Apple, we receive a user identifier and, if you allow it, your display name. We do not receive your password, contacts or posts.

- Purchases: payments are handled by Apple, Google or Huawei. We receive confirmation of the purchase only, never your card details.

- Advertising: the Game shows ads through Google AdMob (and Huawei Ads on Huawei devices). These providers may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address and ad interactions to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU/UK), you are asked for consent first. On iOS you can allow or deny tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt.

How we use information

To save your progress, run leaderboards, deliver purchased items, show ads and keep the Game working and secure. We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices

You can play without signing in. You can reset your ad identifier or limit ad tracking in your device settings. To delete your leaderboard data, email us at support@afterwishinc.com with your player name.
