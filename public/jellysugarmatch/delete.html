Delete your Jelly Sugar Match account

In the game: Settings → Delete Account → Delete. This removes your online account, leaderboard scores, player name and Facebook / Apple login link from our server immediately.

You can also email support@afterwishinc.com from any device with the subject "Delete account" and your player name; we delete the data within 7 days.

Progress stored only on your phone (levels, coins) stays there until you uninstall the Game or use Settings → Reset.

Purchase records are kept by Apple, Google or Huawei under their own policies.
